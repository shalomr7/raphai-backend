# HeartPurse Privacy Policy

**Version:** 3.0
**Effective date:** 10 October 2026
**Last updated:** 10 October 2026

HeartPurse is an Android app that helps you look after your health, fitness, habits and personal money in one place. This Privacy Policy explains, in plain language, what information we collect, why we collect it, how we protect it, how long we keep it, and the choices and rights you have.

If anything here is unclear, please contact us (see "Contact us" at the end).

> **Our promises**
>
> - **We never sell your data.** Not to advertisers, data brokers or anyone else.
> - **No loans.** We never offer loans or credit, and we never use your data to decide credit, lending, insurance or employment.
> - **Your health data is never used for ads.**
> - **You stay in control.** You can export your data or delete your account at any time.

## 3. What we collect

We collect only what you give us, what you allow the app to read from your phone, and the basic records we need to run your account and subscription and keep the service safe.

### 3.1 Account details
- Your name and email address.
- Your password, which we store only in a scrambled, one-way form. We never see or keep your actual password.
- If you ask to reset your password, we send a one-time code to your email.

### 3.2 Profile details
- Sex, age, height, weight, activity level, goals, preferred pace and daily step goal.
- Optional body measurements (such as neck, waist and hip) used to estimate body-fat percentage. The estimate itself is worked out when you view it and is not saved.
- Optional monthly income, used for budgeting.

### 3.3 Health and wellness information you enter
- Food and drink logs, including food you describe in your own words (for example "2 rotis and dal"), custom foods and favourites.
- Water intake, sleep, mood and optional mood notes, weight history and workouts.
- Reminder settings, such as sit-break and water reminders.

Please only write in notes what you are comfortable keeping in the app.

### 3.4 Health and activity data from your phone
With your permission, HeartPurse can read data from **Android Health Connect**, including steps, distance, active calories, exercise sessions, heart rate, resting heart rate, sleep and weight. This may come from your phone or from wearables and other apps that share data with Health Connect.

- You choose which types of data to share, and each is asked for separately.
- If you allow it, HeartPurse can also read this data while the app is closed, so your activity and sleep stay up to date. This is optional.
- If you do not use Health Connect, HeartPurse can count steps using your phone's motion sensor (the "Physical activity" permission).
- The data we read is sent securely to your HeartPurse account, so your history is kept and available if you change phones.

You can change or remove these permissions at any time in your phone's settings (Settings → Health Connect → App permissions → HeartPurse, or Settings → Apps → HeartPurse → Permissions).

### 3.5 Money information you enter
- Expenses (amount, category, how you paid, such as UPI, card or cash, and an optional note).
- Budgets, savings goals, bills, bill payments and monthly income.

What we **do not** collect: card numbers, bank account numbers, UPI IDs, SMS messages, or data from your bank or payment apps. HeartPurse does not connect to your bank and does not move money.

Some tools keep data only on your phone or do not save it at all: bill-split names and amounts stay **on your phone only**, and SIP and EMI calculator inputs are **not saved**.

### 3.6 Insights we work out for you
Using the information above, HeartPurse works out your **HeartScore** (an overall score across areas such as health, fitness and money), daily priorities, patterns in your habits (for example, "on days you sleep 7+ hours you walk more") and a daily brief. These are suggestions to help you, not decisions about you, and they have no legal effect. We save your daily HeartScore so you can see how it changes over time.

### 3.7 Coach questions
The HeartPurse Coach answers your questions using your message and the parts of your profile and logs needed to answer it.

- Today, Coach answers are produced by HeartPurse itself. Your questions are used to work out the answer and are **not saved**. We only keep a count of how many questions you asked each day, so we can apply plan limits fairly.
- **Planned, and only if you switch it on:** smarter Coach answers from a third-party AI provider. This will be **off by default** and will start only after you read a clear notice and turn it on in **You → Privacy Centre**. When it is on, your question and only the summary information needed to answer (for example, your goal and recent totals) are sent to the AI provider, which may process it outside India. You can turn it off at any time.

### 3.8 Subscription and purchase records
All payments are handled by **Google Play**. We never see your card or UPI details. Google Play tells us which plan you bought, its status and when it renews or ends, so we can unlock your plan.

### 3.9 Notifications
If you allow notifications, HeartPurse sends the reminders you set up, such as sit-break, water and bill reminders. You can turn notifications off at any time in your phone's settings.

### 3.10 Security and technical records
To keep your account and the service safe, we keep a record of important account events (such as sign-ins, failed sign-ins, data exports and account deletion), with the time, your IP address and your device or browser type.

We do not use third-party analytics or crash-reporting tools today. If we add one, we will update this policy first.

### 3.11 Ads (Free plan only, planned)
HeartPurse does not show ads today. If we add ads to the Free plan in future, an advertising partner may use your device's advertising ID and basic device information to show ads. See Section 7.

## 5. Your consent and choices

- **We ask before we collect.** When you sign up, and before any optional feature starts, we tell you what we will collect and why, and ask for your agreement.
- **Each optional feature is asked for separately.** Health Connect (each data type and background access), physical activity, notifications, the planned AI Coach and planned personalised ads each have their own choice. Nothing is switched on for you in advance.
- **You can say no.** If you refuse something optional, only the feature that needs it will not work. You can always log things by hand instead.
- **You can change your mind at any time**, as easily as you agreed: use the switches in **You → Privacy Centre**, your phone's permission settings, or email us. We will then stop that use, except where the law requires us to keep something. Withdrawing consent does not affect what was done before.
- **Notice in your language.** You can ask us for this policy in English or any language listed in the Eighth Schedule to the Constitution of India.
- We keep a record of the choices you make (what you agreed to, which version and when) so we can show we have your consent.

## 6. How we treat health data

Health and activity data, including data from Health Connect:

- is used **only** to provide and improve the health and fitness features you see in HeartPurse;
- is **never** used for advertising, ad targeting or ad measurement;
- is **never sold**, and never given to advertisers or data brokers;
- is **never** used to make decisions about credit, lending, insurance or employment;
- is shared only with the service providers who help us run HeartPurse (Section 7), when you ask us to (for example, when you export your data), or when the law requires it;
- is **not viewed by people** at HeartPurse unless you allow it (for example, in a support request), it is needed for security, or the law requires it; and
- is encrypted when it is sent and when it is stored.

HeartPurse follows Google Play's policies for health data and Health Connect.

## 7. Who we share information with

**We do not sell your personal data.** We share it only with trusted service providers who help us run HeartPurse, under contracts that require them to keep it secure and use it only for the work they do for us. They are:

- **Cloud hosting and database providers**, who run our servers and store your account and app data securely.
- **An email delivery provider**, used only to send you account emails such as password-reset codes.
- **App build and update services**, which help us build and deliver the app. They do not receive your health or money data.
- **Google Play**, which handles app delivery, payments and subscriptions.
- **An AI provider** (planned, and only if you switch the AI Coach on), to produce Coach answers.
- **An advertising partner** (planned, Free plan only), to show ads. It never receives your health or money data.

We may also share information without asking you **only where the law requires it**, for example:

- with government agencies authorised by law that make a lawful written request;
- when a court orders it or a law requires it;
- to report a security incident or data breach to the authorities, as the law requires; or
- to protect someone's life or safety in an emergency.

**If the business changes hands.** If HeartPurse is merged, sold or reorganised, your information will only move with the same protections as this policy, and we will tell you first.

## 10. How long we keep your information

We keep information only as long as we need it, or as long as the law requires.

| Information | How long we keep it |
|---|---|
| Account, profile, health, activity, money and insight data | Until you delete it or delete your account |
| After you delete your account | Removed from our active systems straight away. Any copies in short-term backups are removed within 7 days. |
| Coach questions | Not saved. Only a daily count is kept, until you delete your account. |
| Security records | 1 year, then deleted |
| Records of your consent choices | For as long as you have an account, plus 1 year |
| Subscription and purchase records | 8 years, because tax laws require it |
| Support and complaint emails | 1 year after the matter is closed |
| Bill-split and calculator inputs | Never stored on our servers |

**Inactive accounts.** If you do not use HeartPurse or contact us for **3 years**, we may delete your account. We will email you **at least 48 hours before** we do, so you can sign in to keep it.

> **Important:** Deleting your account or uninstalling the app **does not cancel a Google Play subscription.** Please cancel it first in **Google Play → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions**.

## 11. Your rights

Under Indian law, including the DPDP Act, you have the right to:

1. **Access** – get a summary of the information we hold about you, how we use it and who we have shared it with.
2. **Correct** – fix information that is wrong, incomplete or out of date. You can edit most of it yourself in the app.
3. **Delete** – ask us to delete your information, except anything the law requires us to keep.
4. **Withdraw consent** – at any time, as easily as you gave it.
5. **Complain** – raise a complaint with our Grievance Officer (Section 15).
6. **Nominate** – name someone to exercise your rights if you die or become unable to. Email us their name and contact details.
7. **Escalate** – if you are not satisfied with our answer, complain to the **Data Protection Board of India**.

We reply to rights requests within **30 days**.

## 12. How to export your data or delete your account

- **Export your data:** in the app, go to **You → Privacy Centre → Export my data**.
- **Delete your account in the app:** go to **You → Privacy Centre → Delete account**. This deletes your account and all linked data from our servers and clears HeartPurse data from your phone.
- **Delete your account without the app:** use our [account deletion page](/delete-account), or email [SUPPORT EMAIL] from your registered email address with the subject "Delete my HeartPurse account". We will confirm it is you, then delete your account.
- **Phone permissions** (Health Connect, physical activity, notifications): change them in your phone's settings.
- **Anything else** (a copy of your information, corrections, nominations or complaints): email [SUPPORT EMAIL].

## 13. How we protect your information

We use reasonable security practices suited to health and money information, including:

- **Encryption** when your information is sent between the app and our servers, and when it is stored.
- **Strong password protection**: passwords are stored only in a scrambled, one-way form, and your sign-in is kept in your phone's secure storage.
- **Limited access**: only authorised people can reach our systems, and each part of the service gets only the access it needs.
- **Monitoring**: important account and security events are recorded and reviewed to spot and stop misuse.
- **Backups** so your information can be recovered if something goes wrong.
- **Safe development**: we review our code, keep software up to date and fix known security issues.
- **Contracts** that require our service providers to keep your information secure.

No system is completely secure, but we work hard to protect your information.

**If there is a data breach** that affects your information, we will tell you without delay, in plain language, what happened, what it means for you, what we are doing and what you can do. We will also report it to the Indian authorities within the time limits the law sets.
